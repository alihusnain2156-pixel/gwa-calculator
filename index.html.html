How to Compute Your GWA in the Philippines

Your General Weighted Average (GWA) is the single most important number on your transcript. It decides whether you qualify for Latin honors, keep your scholarship, get into graduate school, or meet an employer’s cut-off. Computing it is simple once you understand that it is a weighted average — subjects with more units count more.

Step by step

  • Step 1 — List your subjects. Write down each subject, its final grade, and its credit units.
  • Step 2 — Multiply grade by units. For every subject, multiply the final grade by its units.
  • Step 3 — Add everything up. Sum all the weighted grade points, and separately sum all the units.
  • Step 4 — Divide. Divide the total weighted grade points by the total units. That is your GWA.
GWA = Σ (Grade × Units) ÷ Σ (Total Units)

Worked example

SubjectGradeUnitsGrade × Units
Calculus1.5057.50
Physics1.7547.00
English2.0036.00
Filipino1.2533.75
Total—1524.25

GWA = 24.25 ÷ 15 = 1.62 — which falls in the Cum Laude range at most Philippine universities.

Latin Honors & Dean’s List Requirements

Latin honors are awarded at graduation based on your cumulative GWA, while the Dean’s List is awarded per semester. Most schools also require no failing grades, no unresolved INC, and no major disciplinary record.

Summa Cum Laude1.00 – 1.2096–100%
Magna Cum Laude1.21 – 1.4593–95%
Cum Laude1.46 – 1.7587–92%
Dean’s List (per sem)1.75 or better87%+

At the University of the Philippines, recognitions are University Scholar (1.45 or better) and College Scholar (1.75 or better), each requiring no grade below 3.00 and no INC.

GWA by University

Most Philippine universities — UP, PUP, UST, FEU, NU and Mapúa — use the standard 1.00–5.00 scale. A few exceptions:

  • UP: uses 4.00 as a conditional pass and DRP for dropped subjects (excluded from GWA). Recognition is University / College Scholar.
  • DLSU: uses a 4.0 ascending scale where 4.0 is highest. Select DLSU above for the correct scale.
  • Ateneo (ADMU): uses letter grades mapped to a 4.0 quality-point system.
  • UST: grades internally in percentage but reports on the 1.00–5.00 scale.
  • Mapúa & TIP: use QPI on a 0–4 ascending scale.
  • Senior High School (DepEd): uses a 75%-passing percentage system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you compute GWA in the Philippines?
Multiply each subject’s final grade by its credit units, add the products, then divide by the total units. Formula: GWA = Σ(Grade × Units) ÷ Σ(Units).
What GWA is needed for Cum Laude?
Cum Laude requires 1.46–1.75, Magna Cum Laude 1.21–1.45, and Summa Cum Laude 1.00–1.20 — all with zero failing grades.
Is a 1.75 GWA good?
Yes. A 1.75 GWA (around 87–88%) is the standard Cum Laude and Dean’s List threshold and qualifies for most scholarships.
What is the passing GWA in college?
A grade of 3.00 is the minimum passing grade. A 4.00 is a conditional pass and a 5.00 is failing.
Is GWA the same as GPA?
No. GWA uses the inverted 1.00–5.00 scale (1.00 best). GPA uses the 4.0 scale (4.0 best). Use the converter above.
Can senior high school students use this?
Yes — choose “Senior High School (DepEd)” in the dropdown to use the percentage system.
